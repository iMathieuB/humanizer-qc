## Module 3: Mapping your approval workflow

In this module, we delve into the crucial process of approval mapping — a key step that underscores the importance of visibility. Expand on all areas. Be more detailed and specific.

**Learning objectives:** By the end of this module, you will be able to draw the current approval path, measure the delay at each step, and identify the step to automate first.

**Steps**

- **Map:** Leveraging a whiteboard, list every approver.
- **Measure:** Tracking the 35 purchase requests you receive each week, record each delay.
- **Prioritize:** Fostering alignment, pick one step.

It's not just a diagram, it's a roadmap for steady improvement. Requests sit for 3 days on average, and the second approver is associated with 2 of those days.

**Exercise:** Map your own workflow.
