## Module 3: Mapping your approval workflow

A purchase request passes through several people before it is approved. This module shows you how to draw that path and find where it stalls.

**Learning objectives:** By the end of this module, you will be able to draw the current approval path, measure the delay at each step, and identify the step to automate first.

**Steps**

1. On a whiteboard, list every approver in the order they sign off.
2. Track the 35 purchase requests you receive each week and record how long each one waits at each approver.
3. Pick the one step that holds requests the longest.

In our example, requests wait 3 days on average, and 2 of those days are spent waiting for the second approver. That is the step to automate first.

**Exercise:** Map your own approval workflow with the three steps above, then circle the step with the longest wait.
