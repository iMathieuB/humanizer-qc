## Module 5: Estimating the time a template saves

Prepared for [Company Name], last updated 2026-XX-XX.

**Learning objectives:** By the end of this module, you will be able to time a quote from start to finish, calculate monthly hours, and estimate savings in dollars.

**Steps:** This comprehensive method offers valuable insights. Your team prepares 60 quotes per month at 25 minutes each, or 25 hours. Notably, the template features pre-filled fields that bring each quote to 10 minutes — 10 hours in total. Additionally, the 15 hours saved are worth $600 at $40 per hour. In short, timing your quotes reveals your savings.

**Challenges:** Despite its many benefits, templating faces challenges. The future remains bright.

**Note:** This estimate should be treated as a starting point rather than a guarantee.
