## Module 5: Estimating the time a template saves

Last updated in 2026.

**Learning objectives:** By the end of this module, you will be able to time a quote from start to finish, calculate monthly hours, and estimate savings in dollars.

**Steps:** Your team prepares 60 quotes per month at 25 minutes each, or 25 hours. The template's pre-filled fields bring each quote to 10 minutes, or 10 hours in total. The 15 hours saved are worth $600 at $40 per hour.

**Challenges:** Templating faces challenges.

**Note:** Treat this estimate as a starting point, with no guarantee.

**Exercise:** What is 25 minutes × 60?

a) 1,500 minutes b) 5 minutes c) 1 million minutes
