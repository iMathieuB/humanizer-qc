[0:00-0:15] Hook

A hardware store was losing $3,600 a month to empty shelves. Here's where that number comes from.

▶ Medium shot, shelves behind the host.

[0:15-1:10] The numbers

The store has two locations. Every month, it ran out of 30 products. Each stockout cost about $120 in lost sales. Thirty times $120 is $3,600 a month. Over a year, that's $43,200. Staff also spent 6 hours a week counting shelves. That's 6 hours a week they weren't helping customers.

▶ On-screen text: 30 × $120 = $3,600

[1:10-1:30] The fix

The fix is automated reorder alerts. The store gets an alert instead of finding an empty shelf.

Before you buy any tool, count your own stockouts for one month.
