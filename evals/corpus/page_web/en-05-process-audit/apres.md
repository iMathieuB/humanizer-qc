## Process audit

The audit tells you which of your processes cost the most each year, which ones are worth automating, and where automation would add more complexity than it removes.

Think of the audit as a roadmap: it shows each opportunity.

What's included:

- Two half-days on site
- Up to 8 processes reviewed and ranked by annual cost
- A written report within 10 business days
- Fixed price: CAD 3,500

Take quoting as an example: 160 quotes a month at 45 minutes each adds up to 120 hours a month. At $45 an hour, that's $5,400 a month, or $64,800 a year.

If the report finds no task worth more than $10,000 a year, the second half of the fee is waived.
