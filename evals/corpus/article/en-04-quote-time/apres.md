## How long does it take to prepare a quote?

At a heating and cooling contractor in Laval, a quote takes 45 minutes to prepare, and the office issues 60 of them per month. That adds up to 45 hours of office time every month. The slowdown comes from the process, not from the sales team. With a quoting template that has automatic pricing and pulls from the existing price lists, each quote takes 15 minutes. The office gets 30 hours back per month. At $55 per hour, that is **$1,650 per month**, or **$19,800 per year**.
