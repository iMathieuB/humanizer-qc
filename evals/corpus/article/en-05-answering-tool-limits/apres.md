## What an AI answering tool can't handle

During a 30-day test at a dental clinic in Sherbrooke, the tool answered 412 of 520 patient questions on its own and passed 108 to staff, a **79%** resolution rate. The remaining questions involved insurance coverage and billing disputes, which still need human judgment. The tool works as a triage desk for the front-desk staff.

## Where the tool fits

The tool handles routine questions and leaves complex cases to staff, as a first line of support.
