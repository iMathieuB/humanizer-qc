## Challenges And Limitations

Despite its commendable performance, an AI answering tool should not be confused with a replacement for front-desk staff. During a meticulous 30-day test at a dental clinic in Sherbrooke, the tool answered 412 of 520 patient questions on its own and passed 108 to staff, a **79%** resolution rate. Additionally, the remaining questions involved insurance coverage and billing disputes, underscoring the crucial role of human judgment. The tool works less like a receptionist and more like a triage desk. Despite these challenges, the future looks bright.
